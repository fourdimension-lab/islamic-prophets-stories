Historical Sources & Verification

All 100 accounts presented on this portal are drawn directly from canonical Islamic literature:

  • The Holy Quran (Surahs Al-Baqarah, Al-Imran, Al-A'raf, Yusuf, Al-Kahf, Maryam, Al-Anbiya, and others)
  • Tafsir Ibn Kathir (Stories of the Prophets / Qasas al-Anbiya)
  • Sahih al-Bukhari & Sahih Muslim
  • Al-Bidayah wan-Nihayah by Ibn Kathir
  • Siyar A'lam an-Nubala by Imam adh-Dhahabi
  • Al-Isabah fi Tamyiz al-Sahabah by Ibn Hajar al-Asqalani

Care is taken to avoid fabricated narrations (Isra'iliyyat) and present authentic, consensus-backed accounts.
